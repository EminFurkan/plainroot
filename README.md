# Plainroot Studio

The public pages for Plainroot Studio's apps, served by Cloudflare at https://plainrootstudio.com. Every push to `main` deploys.

- `lexmio/index.html`: the Lexmio page in English and Turkish, at https://plainrootstudio.com/lexmio (the App Store marketing URL)
- `lexmio/support.html`: Lexmio support, at https://plainrootstudio.com/lexmio/support
- `lexmio/privacy.html`: Lexmio privacy policy, at https://plainrootstudio.com/lexmio/privacy

The Lexmio pages are generated from `Site/` in the Lexmio project, with the support address filled in and links without the `.html` ending. Edit them there and copy them here, so the app and these pages stay in step.

The Lexmio page is kept here, not in the Lexmio project, because the app doesn't link to it. Its screenshots in `lexmio/img/` and the press kit in `lexmio/press/` come from the Lexmio project's `Docs/Evidence/Store-1.0/raw/` captures and its app icon. Its headings use Fraunces from the app, subset to Latin and Turkish in `lexmio/fonts/` with its SIL Open Font License beside it. At release, replace the "Coming to the App Store" line in both languages with Apple's App Store badge linking to https://apps.apple.com/app/id6822015059.
