Paperhop Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

In short: Paperhop is for parents. Your children’s drawings, names, ages and recordings stay on your devices and in your family’s iCloud. A drawing leaves your iPhone only when you bring it to life or make a style, and only after you agree on the consent screen. Then the drawing and its description go to our server and to the AI services that make the video or picture, and our server deletes them within 24 hours. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Paperhop is made by Plainroot Studio (“we”). This policy covers the Paperhop app for iPhone and its widgets.

What stays with your family

Your drawings and their titles, your children’s names and birth years and months, the recordings and the words written from them, your favourites, and the videos and pictures you’ve made are kept on your iPhone. If iCloud is on for Paperhop, they also sync through your family’s private iCloud to the devices signed in to the same Apple Account. That storage is Apple’s, and we can’t read it.

A recording is written out on your iPhone. The recording itself never goes to us or to AI services.

The widgets show a copy of recent drawings and favourites kept on your iPhone.

What the app sends, and when

Only when you tap Bring it to life or make a style, and only after you’ve agreed on the consent screen, the app sends our server:

  • the drawing, as a picture at most 1280 pixels on its longer side, with no photo metadata such as location;
  • its description, as you left it on the Animate screen (up to 300 characters), and which language it’s in;
  • the look you chose.

It never sends your child’s name, age, birthday or recording. The description is sent as written, so it’s best to leave names and other personal details out of it.

The AI services

Our server runs on Cloudflare. It passes the drawing and its description to fal (fal.ai), which runs the AI models Paperhop uses:

  • Google’s Gemini checks that it’s a hand-made drawing with no real person or photo in it, and writes the movement from the description. fal reaches it through OpenRouter, a routing service.
  • OpenAI’s GPT Image makes the keepsake styles, and the picture that Made real, Plush toy and Clay videos start from.
  • Kuaishou’s Kling makes the video.

These services get only the drawing and text based on its description, sent from our server. They get nothing from your iPhone, and nothing that names you or your child.

How long it’s kept

  • Our server deletes the drawing, its description and what’s made from it within 24 hours, usually as soon as your iPhone has downloaded the result. A record of each job, with no picture, description or prompt, is deleted after 30 days.
  • fal keeps no history of our requests, and the files it makes expire within an hour. Our server deletes them as soon as it has its own copy.
  • Google may keep requests for a limited time to detect abuse: up to 55 days, or up to 90 days for anything its safety checks flag. It doesn’t use them to train its models.
  • OpenAI may keep requests for up to 30 days to detect abuse, and longer only where the law requires it or to prevent harm. It doesn’t use them to train its models.
  • Kuaishou, through Kling AI Pte. Ltd., stores data on its servers in Singapore and keeps it as long as it needs to provide its service. Its privacy policy says it doesn’t use it for model training.

What our server keeps for your plan

To know which plan you have and how many videos and keepsake styles are left, our server keeps:

  • an ID that Apple gives your Apple Account for Paperhop. It isn’t your name, your email or your Apple Account itself, and the devices on that Apple Account share it;
  • for each iPhone, a key from Apple’s App Attest, which proves that requests come from the real Paperhop app, and its notification token if you allow notifications;
  • your Paperhop purchases as Apple signs them: the product, its dates, the App Store country, whether it’s in the free week, and any refund;
  • a record of the videos and keepsake styles your plan has given, used and given back.

Each request also carries the app’s version number. We keep these records while they’re needed for your plan and your videos.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, our server uses your iPhone’s notification token, through Apple, to tell you when a video is ready or couldn’t be made. The messages are generic, such as “Your drawing is alive”, and never include the drawing or its title.

Purchases

Paperhop Plus and video packs are bought through Apple, under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. We never receive your payment details. The app sends our server Apple’s signed record of each purchase, so that you get the right number of videos and keepsake styles.

Logs

Our server’s database never stores IP addresses. Cloudflare, which runs our server, keeps request logs for up to 7 days: the time of each request, what it asked for and technical details of the connection, which can include the IP address. Our own log lines record only IDs, statuses, timings, models and costs, never a drawing, a description or a prompt.

Sharing and saving

Reveal reels, videos, pictures and the year book PDF are made on your iPhone, and go only where you share or save them. A reveal reel can include your child’s recording. Videos and pictures carry a small “Made with Paperhop” mark, which also tells viewers they were made with AI; with Plus, you can turn it off in Settings.

Your choices

Settings → AI consent → Turn off AI consent stops the app sending drawings. Scanning and the fridge still work. You can turn notifications off in your iPhone’s Settings.

Deleting your data

Delete a drawing from its page, or a child and their drawings in Settings → Children; it’s removed from your iCloud and from your family’s devices. Deleting the app removes what it stored on that iPhone. To remove Paperhop’s data from iCloud as well, open your iPhone’s Settings → your name → iCloud → Storage and choose Paperhop.

Our server deletes drawings and what’s made from them within 24 hours on its own. To have the records about your Apple Account deleted, write to us. Paperhop has no account, so we can’t look you up by your name or email; we’ll work out with you how to find them.

Children

Paperhop is for parents. Children draw, talk about their drawings and watch them come alive, but a parent sets the app up, agrees to the consent screen and makes every purchase. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children: their names, ages and recordings never reach us, and a drawing and its description reach us only after a parent agrees, and are deleted within 24 hours.

Where it’s processed

Our server runs on Cloudflare’s worldwide network. fal, Google and OpenAI process data in the United States and other countries, and Kuaishou in Singapore, so a drawing and its description may be processed outside your country.

No ads, no tracking

Paperhop has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn’t use Apple’s advertising identifier, and we never sell your data or share it for advertising.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us what we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it, and you can complain to your data protection authority. To do any of this, write to us.

Test versions (TestFlight)

If you test a pre-release version through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, and feedback and screenshots you choose to send, with us, as described in Apple’s TestFlight terms. We use them only to improve the app.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for that.

Changes

If Paperhop starts to handle data differently, for example by sending something new or using a new service, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released, and the consent screen will say so.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@plainrootstudio.com

Uygulama, yalnızca Canlandır’a dokunduğunuzda ya da bir stil oluşturduğunuzda ve yalnızca izin ekranında onay verdikten sonra sunucumuza şunları gönderir:

Çocuğunuzun adını, yaşını, doğum gününü ya da ses kaydını hiçbir zaman göndermez. Açıklama yazıldığı gibi gönderilir; bu yüzden içine adları ve başka kişisel bilgileri yazmamanız en iyisidir.

Yapay zekâ hizmetleri

Sunucumuz Cloudflare üzerinde çalışır. Resmi ve açıklamasını, Paperhop’un kullandığı yapay zekâ modellerini çalıştıran fal’a (fal.ai) iletir:

  • OpenAI’ın GPT Image modeli hatıra stillerini ve Gerçek gibi, Peluş oyuncak ve Kil videolarının başladığı görseli üretir.
  • Kuaishou’nun Kling modeli videoyu üretir.

Bu hizmetler yalnızca resmi ve açıklamasına dayanan metni, sunucumuzdan alır. iPhone’unuzdan hiçbir şey ve sizi ya da çocuğunuzu adıyla tanıtan hiçbir bilgi almazlar.

En resumen: Paperhop es para padres. Los dibujos de tus hijos, sus nombres, sus edades y sus grabaciones se quedan en tus dispositivos y en el iCloud de tu familia. Un dibujo solo sale de tu iPhone cuando le das vida o creas un estilo, y solo después de que lo aceptes en la pantalla de permiso. Entonces, el dibujo y su descripción van a nuestro servidor y a los servicios de IA que crean el vídeo o la imagen, y nuestro servidor los elimina en un plazo de 24 horas. No hay anuncios, ni analíticas, ni seguimiento.

Quiénes somos

Paperhop es una app de Plainroot Studio («nosotros»). Esta política se aplica a la app Paperhop para iPhone y a sus widgets.

Lo que se queda con tu familia

Tus dibujos y sus títulos, los nombres de tus hijos con su año y mes de nacimiento, las grabaciones y las palabras que se transcriben de ellas, tus favoritos, y los vídeos y las imágenes que has creado se guardan en tu iPhone. Si iCloud está activado para Paperhop, también se sincronizan a través del iCloud privado de tu familia con los dispositivos que usan la misma cuenta de Apple. Ese almacenamiento es de Apple y nosotros no podemos leerlo.

Las grabaciones se pasan a texto en tu iPhone. La grabación en sí nunca se envía, ni a nosotros ni a los servicios de IA.

Los widgets muestran una copia de los dibujos y favoritos recientes guardada en tu iPhone.

Qué envía la app y cuándo

Solo cuando tocas Darle vida o creas un estilo, y solo después de aceptar en la pantalla de permiso, la app envía a nuestro servidor:

  • el dibujo, como imagen de 1280 píxeles como máximo en su lado más largo, sin metadatos de foto como la ubicación;
  • su descripción, tal como la dejaste en la pantalla Animar (hasta 300 caracteres), y el idioma en que está;
  • el aspecto que elegiste.

Nunca envía el nombre, la edad, el cumpleaños ni la grabación de tu hijo. La descripción se envía tal como está escrita, así que es mejor no poner en ella nombres ni otros datos personales.

Los servicios de IA

Nuestro servidor funciona en Cloudflare. Pasa el dibujo y su descripción a fal (fal.ai), que ejecuta los modelos de IA que usa Paperhop:

  • Gemini, de Google, comprueba que es un dibujo hecho a mano, sin ninguna persona real ni foto, y escribe el movimiento a partir de la descripción. fal accede a él a través de OpenRouter, un servicio de enrutamiento.
  • GPT Image, de OpenAI, crea los estilos de recuerdo y la imagen de la que parten los vídeos Como de verdad, Peluche y Plastilina.
  • Kling, de Kuaishou, crea el vídeo.

Estos servicios solo reciben el dibujo y un texto basado en su descripción, enviados desde nuestro servidor. No reciben nada de tu iPhone, ni nada con tu nombre o el de tu hijo.

Cuánto tiempo se conserva

  • Nuestro servidor elimina el dibujo, su descripción y lo que se crea a partir de él en un plazo de 24 horas, normalmente en cuanto tu iPhone ha descargado el resultado. Un registro de cada trabajo, sin imagen, descripción ni instrucciones para la IA, se elimina a los 30 días.
  • fal no guarda un historial de nuestras solicitudes, y los archivos que crea caducan en menos de una hora. Nuestro servidor los elimina en cuanto tiene su propia copia.
  • Google puede conservar las solicitudes durante un tiempo limitado para detectar abusos: hasta 55 días, o hasta 90 días si sus controles de seguridad marcan algo. No las usa para entrenar sus modelos.
  • OpenAI puede conservar las solicitudes hasta 30 días para detectar abusos, y más tiempo solo cuando la ley lo exige o para evitar daños. No las usa para entrenar sus modelos.
  • Kuaishou, a través de Kling AI Pte. Ltd., guarda los datos en sus servidores de Singapur y los conserva mientras los necesita para prestar su servicio. Su política de privacidad dice que no los usa para entrenar modelos.

Lo que guarda nuestro servidor para tu plan

Para saber qué plan tienes y cuántos vídeos y estilos de recuerdo te quedan, nuestro servidor guarda:

  • un identificador que Apple asigna a tu cuenta de Apple para Paperhop. No es tu nombre, ni tu correo, ni tu cuenta de Apple en sí, y lo comparten los dispositivos de esa cuenta de Apple;
  • para cada iPhone, una clave de App Attest de Apple, que demuestra que las solicitudes vienen de la app Paperhop auténtica, y su token de notificaciones si permites las notificaciones;
  • tus compras de Paperhop tal como Apple las firma: el producto, sus fechas, el país de la App Store, si está en la semana gratis y cualquier reembolso;
  • un registro de los vídeos y estilos de recuerdo que tu plan ha dado, usado y devuelto.

Cada solicitud incluye también el número de versión de la app. Conservamos estos registros mientras hagan falta para tu plan y tus vídeos.

Notificaciones

Si permites las notificaciones, nuestro servidor usa el token de notificaciones de tu iPhone, a través de Apple, para avisarte cuando un vídeo está listo o no se ha podido crear. Los mensajes son genéricos, como «Tu dibujo ya tiene vida», y nunca incluyen el dibujo ni su título.

Compras

Paperhop Plus y los paquetes de vídeos se compran a través de Apple, con las condiciones y la política de privacidad de Apple. Nunca recibimos tus datos de pago. La app envía a nuestro servidor el registro firmado por Apple de cada compra, para que recibas el número correcto de vídeos y estilos de recuerdo.

Registros

La base de datos de nuestro servidor nunca guarda direcciones IP. Cloudflare, donde funciona nuestro servidor, conserva registros de solicitudes hasta 7 días: la hora de cada solicitud, qué pidió y detalles técnicos de la conexión, que pueden incluir la dirección IP. Nuestros propios registros solo anotan identificadores, estados, tiempos, modelos y costes, nunca un dibujo, una descripción ni instrucciones para la IA.

Compartir y guardar

Los vídeos revelación, los vídeos, las imágenes y el PDF del álbum del año se crean en tu iPhone, y solo van adonde los compartas o los guardes. Un vídeo revelación puede incluir la grabación de tu hijo. Los vídeos y las imágenes llevan una pequeña marca «Hecho con Paperhop», que también indica a quien los ve que se crearon con IA; con Plus, puedes quitarla en Ajustes.

Tus opciones

Ajustes → Permiso de IA → Retirar el permiso de IA hace que la app deje de enviar dibujos. El escaneo y la nevera siguen funcionando. Puedes desactivar las notificaciones en los Ajustes de tu iPhone.

Eliminar tus datos

Chi siamo

Paperhop è realizzata da Plainroot Studio («noi»). Questa informativa riguarda l’app Paperhop per iPhone e i suoi widget.

Cosa resta alla tua famiglia

I tuoi disegni e i loro titoli, i nomi dei tuoi figli con anno e mese di nascita, le registrazioni e le parole trascritte da esse, i tuoi preferiti, e i video e le immagini che hai creato sono conservati sul tuo iPhone. Se iCloud è attivo per Paperhop, vengono anche sincronizzati tramite l’iCloud privato della tua famiglia con i dispositivi su cui hai effettuato l’accesso con lo stesso Apple Account. Quello spazio è di Apple e noi non possiamo leggerlo.

Una registrazione viene trascritta sul tuo iPhone. La registrazione in sé non arriva mai a noi né ai servizi di IA.

I widget mostrano una copia dei disegni e dei preferiti recenti, conservata sul tuo iPhone.

Cosa invia l’app, e quando

Solo quando tocchi Dagli vita o crei uno stile, e solo dopo che hai dato il consenso nella schermata del consenso, l’app invia al nostro server:

  • il disegno, come immagine di massimo 1280 pixel sul lato più lungo, senza metadati della foto come la posizione;

  • il look che hai scelto.

Non invia mai il nome, l’età, il compleanno o la registrazione di tuo figlio. La descrizione viene inviata così come è scritta, quindi è meglio non inserire nomi o altri dati personali.

I servizi di IA

Il nostro server funziona su Cloudflare. Passa il disegno e la sua descrizione a fal (fal.ai), che esegue i modelli di IA usati da Paperhop:

  • GPT Image di OpenAI crea gli stili ricordo e l’immagine da cui partono i video Sembra vero, Peluche e Plastilina.
  • Kling di Kuaishou crea il video.

Questi servizi ricevono solo il disegno e un testo basato sulla sua descrizione, inviati dal nostro server. Non ricevono nulla dal tuo iPhone, né nulla che riporti il tuo nome o quello di tuo figlio.

Per quanto tempo viene conservato

  • Il nostro server elimina il disegno, la sua descrizione e ciò che ne viene creato entro 24 ore, di solito appena il tuo iPhone ha scaricato il risultato. Un registro di ogni lavoro, senza immagine, descrizione né prompt, viene eliminato dopo 30 giorni.
