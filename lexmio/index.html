Lexmio for iPhone

English words on your Lock Screen

Every time you check the time, there’s a useful word and its meaning. Open it for an example and how it sounds, and it comes back on day 3 and day 7 to check it stuck.

Coming to the App Store in late October

Free to download · iPhone with iOS 17 or later · In English and Turkish

Learn in moments you already have

  1. 1

    Add a widget

    Put Lexmio below the clock on your Lock Screen, or on your Home Screen.

  2. 2

    Glance through the day

    A word and its meaning are there whenever you pick up your phone, and new ones arrive through the day.

  3. 3

    Make it stick

    Open a word for its example and pronunciation. It comes back on day 3 and day 7 to check you still know it.

A closer look

  • Today’s words, with a meaning, an example and how each one sounds

  • Fill in the blank from the word’s own example, with a letter hint · Plus

  • A practice path in sets of ten, with a level check every five sets · Plus

  • Your streak, your week and the words you’ve met

  • Decks for everyday English, work, travel, academic study and exams

Explained your way

Choose how words are explained: a short meaning in simple English, or a Turkish meaning with translated examples. The app itself is in English and Turkish.

Start at your level, from A1 to C2, or take the one-minute word check. When words feel easy, Lexmio suggests moving up.

A streak that survives a busy day

Every day has a goal: 5 words on the free plan, up to 30 with Plus. Three words keep your streak, and one missed day a week is a rest day, so a busy day never breaks it.

Every word you look at, answer or mark on a widget counts. Mio the fox cheers each milestone.

Free to start

Free

  • Everyday English at any level, A1 to C2: 2,471 words, 5 new ones a day
  • Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets, and a progress widget with your goal and streak
  • Word cards with examples and pronunciation, saved words, the daily goal and the streak
  • Every word comes back on day 3 and day 7 to check it stuck

Lexmio Plus

  • Every deck: IELTS, TOEFL, Academic, Work and Travel, core lists for the SAT and GRE, and a core YDS / YÖKDİL list with Turkish explanations
  • Up to 50 new words a day, with a daily goal of 5 to 30
  • The practice path: fill in the blank with letter hints, stars and a level check every five sets
  • Reviews that bring words back after 2 weeks, a month and beyond
  • Interactive widgets: show the meaning, then Know it or Again, plus widgets for saved words or words due for review

Starts with a free week on the yearly plan, for new subscribers. Monthly and one-time lifetime options too.

Nothing to sign up for

No account, no ads, no tracking. Lexmio works offline, and your words and progress stay on your iPhone. It collects no data.

Yoğun bir günde bile bozulmayan seri

Her günün bir hedefi var: ücretsiz planda 5 kelime, Plus ile 30’a kadar. Serin için günde 3 kelime yeter; haftada bir kaçırdığın gün dinlenme günü sayılır, yoğun bir gün serini bozmaz.

Baktığın, cevapladığın ya da widget’ta işaretlediğin her kelime sayılır. Tilki Mio her dönüm noktasında seninle sevinir.

  • A1’den C2’ye her seviyede Günlük İngilizce: 2.471 kelime, günde 5 yeni kelime
  • Kilit Ekranı ve Ana Ekran widget’ları; hedefini ve serini gösteren ilerleme widget’ı

Lexmio Plus

  • Tüm desteler: Türkçe açıklamalı YDS / YÖKDİL temel listesi, IELTS, TOEFL, Akademik, İş ve Seyahat; SAT ve GRE için temel listeler
  • Günde 50 yeni kelimeye kadar; 5 ile 30 arasında günlük hedef
